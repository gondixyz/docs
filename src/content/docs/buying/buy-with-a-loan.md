---
title: "Buy an NFT with a loan"
description: "Buy now, pay later for NFTs: pay part of the price today, borrow the rest from GONDI lenders and repay by the due date."
---

Pay part of an NFT's price today and borrow the rest from GONDI lenders, in the same purchase. You repay the loan plus interest by its due date.

## How it works

1. Open a listed NFT and click **Buy with Loan**. You'll see the loan offers lenders have made for it.
2. Choose how much to pay today. The loan covers the rest. You'll see the APR, the duration and any origination fee.
3. Review what you pay now and what you'll owe, then confirm in your wallet.

The seller gets paid in full, your loan starts, and the NFT is held as collateral until you repay. All in one transaction.

## An example

A 10 ETH listing, with a 7 WETH loan at 15% APR for 90 days and a 0.20 WETH origination fee:

* **Today** you pay 3.40 ETH plus gas. That's 10 − 7 + 0.20 origination + 0.20 fee (2% of the price).
* **In 90 days** you repay about 7.26 WETH.

## Repaying

Interest only runs while the loan is open, so repaying early costs less. You repay the whole loan at once. Want to sell before it's due? List it with [Sell & Repay](/gondi-v3/sell-and-repay/) and the sale pays off the loan.

If you don't repay by the due date, the loan defaults and you lose the NFT. See [Liquidations, Buyouts & Auctions](/gondi-v3/liquidations-buyouts-and-auctions/).

## Fees

2% of the price. On a Sell & Repay listing it's only a 0.05% flash-loan fee. The lender sets the interest and any origination fee. See [Protocol Fees](/gondi-v3/protocol-fees/).

## What you can buy this way

NFTs listed on GONDI, OpenSea or the CryptoPunks marketplace that a lender has made an offer for, one at a time. ERC-1155 tokens aren't supported.
