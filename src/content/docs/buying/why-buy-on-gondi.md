The best thing about buying on GONDI is that you know you're not buying a counterfeit. Every collection is checked and added by hand.

## Verified collections

We have more than 770 collections (October 2026), picked for trading volume and established artists. That includes thousands of works minted on SuperRare and Foundation, and we add new mints from those contracts by hand too. Copycats and spam don't get in.

Missing a collection? Ask us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/gondi).

## Every listing in one place

Collection pages show listings from OpenSea, the CryptoPunks marketplace, TokenWorks and GONDI, so you can compare and buy from one page.

## No buyer fee

You pay the listed price plus gas. If you buy by placing a bid, it's 0.5% when your bid is accepted. All fees are in [Protocol Fees](/gondi-v3/protocol-fees/).

## Buy now, pay later

Pay part of the price today and borrow the rest from GONDI lenders in the same purchase. The fee is 2%. It works on GONDI, OpenSea and CryptoPunks listings, but not on ERC-1155 tokens. See [Buy an NFT with a loan](/buying/buy-with-a-loan/).

## NFTs that are in a loan

You can buy an NFT that's collateral in a loan. Your purchase repays the loan and the NFT comes to you clean. That's [Sell & Repay](/gondi-v3/sell-and-repay/), and it's how XCOPY's 1/1 *Dissolution* sold on GONDI for $525K.

## 1/1 art

Artist pages show each artist's **Catalogue**: their 1/1s, series and editions, with prices where they're listed.
