---
title: "How to buy an NFT on GONDI"
description: "Connect a wallet, find a verified collection and buy an NFT, sweep up to 30 at once, or place a bid."
---

Connect your wallet on [gondi.xyz](https://www.gondi.xyz), open the NFT, click **Buy Now** and confirm in your wallet. That's it.

## What you need

* A wallet like MetaMask, Rabby, Coinbase Wallet or any WalletConnect wallet. Signing in is a signature, so it costs no gas.
* The listing price in the listing's currency, plus ETH (or the chain's token) for gas. GONDI runs on Ethereum, HyperEVM and Robinhood Chain, and the app switches your wallet to the right one.

## Buy one NFT

1. Search for the collection on [gondi.xyz](https://www.gondi.xyz) or find it in the [collection directory](/gondi-v3/whitelisted-collections/).
2. Open the NFT. You'll see its price and where it's listed. On desktop you can also hover over it on the collection page and click **Buy**.
3. Click **Buy Now**. We check the listing first. If the price changed, you'll see the new one before you accept it.
4. Confirm in your wallet. If the listing is in WETH or USDC, your wallet asks you to approve the token first.

Want to pay only part of it now? Click **Buy with Loan**. See [Buy an NFT with a loan](/buying/buy-with-a-loan/).

## Sweep

On desktop, use the checkout bar at the bottom of a collection page. Click **+** to add the cheapest listings one by one, or select the NFTs you want. You can sweep up to 30 at once, all in the same currency. Click **Buy**, check the total and confirm. If someone buys one before you, we'll tell you which ones you didn't get.

On a phone, tap **Sweep** in the collection toolbar, pick how many with the slider and tap **Buy**.

CryptoPunks marketplace listings and NFTs that are in a loan can't be swept. Buy those one at a time.

## Place a bid

* **On one NFT:** click **Make Buy Offer** on its page, or **Make Stealth Offer** so only the owner sees it.
* **On a collection:** open the **Offer** menu in the checkout bar and choose **Collection buy offer**. Set a price, how many you want and when it expires.
* **On traits:** select traits in the filters, then choose **Trait buy offer**.

A bid is just a signature. Your funds stay in your wallet until someone accepts, and you pay 0.5% when they do.

## Can't buy it?

* It sold or was delisted.
* You don't have enough of the listing's currency, or gas.
* The collection only lets approved contracts move its NFTs. See [ERC-721C Whitelisting](/gondi-v3/erc-721c-whitelisting/).
