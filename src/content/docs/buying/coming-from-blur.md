Blur's website goes offline on November 12, 2026, and Blend stops taking new loans the same day ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/380802/nft-marketplace-blur-to-shut-down-website-stop-new-blend-loans-next-month)). If you traded or borrowed on Blur, here's how to keep doing it on GONDI.

## Move your Blend loans to GONDI

If you have open loans on Blend, connect your wallet on [gondi.xyz](https://www.gondi.xyz) and go to your profile. You'll see a **Migration from Blur** tab with your Blend loans.

1. Click **Move to Gondi** on a loan. It opens the NFT with the loan offers GONDI lenders have made on it.
2. Pick an offer. Before you confirm you'll see the Blur debt, the new loan, the fees and what you pay now, if the new loan doesn't cover the whole debt.
3. Confirm. The GONDI loan repays Blend and starts in the same transaction, and your NFT becomes its collateral. You don't have to sell or repay anything first.

The migration fee is 2% of the Blur debt repaid. If nobody is offering loans on your collection yet, let us know on [Discord](https://discord.gg/gondi).

## Bids, sweeps and selling

| On Blur | On GONDI |
| --- | --- |
| Collection bids | Collection buy offers. Set a price, how many you want and when it expires. |
| Trait bids | Trait buy offers. Pick one or more traits in the filters and bid. |
| Sweeps | Sweep up to 30 NFTs at once from the checkout bar on a collection page. |
| Accepting bids | Select your NFTs in **Sell** mode and accept the top bids in one go. |
| Blend loans | Peer to peer loans with a fixed rate and a due date. See [Loan Basics](/gondi-v3/loan-basics/). |

You also get [stealth bids](/gondi-v3/buy-and-sell-on-gondi/#advanced-bidding), which only the owner sees, and [off-market listings](/gondi-v3/off-market-listings/), which fill when a bid matches without the NFT ever showing as listed.

## What's different

* **Every collection is verified.** We add each one by hand, more than 770 so far, picked for trading volume and established artists, SuperRare and Foundation 1/1s included. You know you're not buying a counterfeit.
* **All the listings in one place.** OpenSea, the CryptoPunks marketplace, TokenWorks and GONDI.
* **No buyer fee on listings.** Sellers pay 0.5% on GONDI listings, and bidders pay 0.5% when a bid is accepted. See [Protocol Fees](/gondi-v3/protocol-fees/).
* **Loans have a due date.** Lenders can't call a GONDI loan early. Repay it by the due date or the lender can claim the NFT.
