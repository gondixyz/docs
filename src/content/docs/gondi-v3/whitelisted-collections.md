Find NFT collections listed on GONDI for trading and lending. Each collection name links to its page in the [GONDI app](https://www.gondi.xyz/collections), where you can check the latest listings, loan offers and item eligibility.

This directory is a dated snapshot of GONDI's public collection catalog. It includes collection subsets and wrappers, which can share a name or contract address. The totals count directory entries, not unique contracts. GONDI user-vault contracts are not included.

**Enabled** means the collection's actions are enabled in the GONDI app. Individual NFTs can still be ineligible, and a collection may have no active listings or loan offers.

**Currently disabled** means the collection remains in the public catalog but its actions are disabled. Always check the collection and item pages before making an offer or starting a transaction.

For the lending process, read [Loan Basics](/gondi-v3/loan-basics/) and [Loan Offers](/gondi-v3/loan-offers/). Some assets use a wrapper; [Vaults & Stash](/gondi-v3/vaults-and-stash/) explains how wrappers and vaults work. Collection links may open a shared page for the original asset and its wrappers.
