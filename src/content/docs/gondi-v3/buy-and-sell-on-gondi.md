## ERC-1155 Trading

Buy, list and make item-specific offers on individual ERC-1155 units without vaulting them first. Each supported order transfers one unit of a specified token ID. See [ERC-1155 Trading](/gondi-v3/erc-1155-trading/) for ownership, approval and settlement requirements.

## Selling on Gondi

**Standard Listings**

See Protocol Fees for full fee details.

* **Public listings**: visible to all buyers
* **Private listings**: restricted to a specific wallet address
* **Off-Market listings:** set a private asking price without publicly showing the NFT as listed. If a copatible bid meets or exceeds your ask, the sale executes.

**Sell & Repay**

Sell & Repay lets you list collateralized NFTs directly. When the sale closes, your loan is automatically repaid and any surplus is sent straight to your wallet. One transaction, fully settled.

## Advanced Bidding

**Stealth Bidding**

**Exclusive on GONDI.** Place a bid that's completely invisible to everyone except the NFT owner. Your wallet address, balance, and bid history stay hidden.

**Multi-Item Bidding**

Place a single bid that can be filled by *any* NFT from a curated set. Perfect for collectors building positions across multiple items in a collection. Set your price once and let the best seller come to you.

**Trait & Multi-Trait Bidding**

Bid on exactly the attributes you want. Target specific traits (e.g., any CryptoPunk with a "Beanie") or stack multiple trait requirements together (e.g., "Zombie + Earring + Cigarette"). Your offer fills only when an NFT matching every criterion is sold to you.

**Edition Bidding**

Place one buy offer covering the eligible ERC-721 copies of a particular edition. Set a price per copy, expiration and capacity: capacity limits how many copies can fill the offer. Edition bidding must be enabled for that edition. For ERC-1155 editions, use an item-specific bid for one unit of the token ID instead.

### Offers Across an Artist's Works

You can select eligible ERC-721 works by an artist across multiple collections on the same chain, including individual 1/1s and ERC-721 edition copies, and publish bids together. Each selected NFT has its own bid price; the bids share a currency and expiration and can be signed with one bulk signature after any required token approval.

This creates **a separate offer for each selected NFT**. Multiple sellers can accept their respective offers; accepting one does not cancel the others. It is not a single offer that fills with any one work by the artist, and it does not automatically include unselected works or future mints. The artist selection supports up to 50 eligible ERC-721 items; ERC-1155 units use individual item bids.

Your potential purchase spend is the sum of the bids plus applicable fees, because every accepted bid is payable separately. Funds remain in your wallet until settlement, and each fill needs sufficient balance and allowance. You cannot select NFTs held by your linked wallets or securing their active loans.

## Fee & Royalty Configuration

**Broker Fees**

Power users, OTC desks, and aggregators can route trades through Gondi with built-in broker support:

* Specify a broker wallet address and fee percentage on any sale
* Fees are distributed automatically on settlement, with no manual reconciliation

**Creator Royalties**

Royalties are fully optional and configurable:

* Set custom royalty rates per listing
* Payments flow automatically to creator wallets at settlement
* Honor creator economics on your terms
